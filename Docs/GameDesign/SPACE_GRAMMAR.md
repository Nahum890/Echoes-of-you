### 1. PURPOSE
Defines spatial proportions, volumetric ratios, compression-release deltas, and visual sightlines to evoke nostalgia, isolation, and liminality in *Echoes of You 2.0*.

### 2. SCOPE
Applies to level layout generation in `EchoesNewProductionBuilder.cs` and spatial layout auditing in `LevelValidator.cs`.

### 3. AUTHORITY
Nivel 2 (Contexto Técnico y Filosofía). Subordinate to `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` (`SPEC-000`) and `DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY.md` (`SPEC-001`). Consolidates `SPATIAL_LANGUAGE.md` and `SHAPE_LANGUAGE.md`.

### 4. DEFINITIONS
- `Compression Volume`: Low ceiling transition space ($H = 3.2\text{m}$) building tension.
- `Release Volume`: High ceiling focal space ($H \ge 5.0\text{m}$) evoking resolution or vulnerability.
- `Volumetric Delta`: Vertical height change $\Delta H = H_{release} - H_{compression}$.

### 7. RULES

- `[RULE-SPA-001]`: **Compression to Release Threshold**: Every level MUST include at least 1 transition from a Compression volume ($H=3.2\text{m}$) to a Release volume ($H \ge 5.0\text{m}$) with vertical delta $\Delta H \ge 1.8\text{m}$.
- `[RULE-SPA-002]`: **Orthogonal Architecture Constraint**: All structural room walls MUST form $90.0^\circ$ or $180.0^\circ$ angles in 2D floorplan space. Curved or organic walls are prohibited.
- `[RULE-SPA-003]`: **Clearance Corridor Floor**: Navigation paths MUST maintain minimum width $W_{clearance} \ge 1.2\text{m}$ along primary circulation routes.
