# ARCHITECTURE_GRAMMAR.md — Spatial Grammar & Structural Architecture
## Spec ID: SPEC-003
## Version: 3.0 (AI-Executable)

---

### 1. PURPOSE
Specifies the geometric, volumetric, topological, and circulation rules for assembling liminal school levels. Defines spatial hierarchy, height matrices, turn intervals, and access sightlines.

### 2. SCOPE
Applies to level layouts generated by `EchoesNewProductionBuilder.cs`, `LevelBlueprint` assets, room modules (`ModuleType`), and architectural prefabs. Excludes prop dressing and URP post-processing.

### 3. AUTHORITY
Nivel 3 (Especificación Ejecutable). Subordinate to `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` (`SPEC-000`) and `DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY.md` (`SPEC-001`).

### 4. DEFINITIONS
- `Dominant Space`: Primary volumetric focal point of a chapter (e.g. Hall $H=5.0\text{m}$, Gym $H=6.0\text{m}$).
- `Secondary Space`: Functional room module (e.g. Classroom $H=3.8\text{m}$, Lab $H=3.8\text{m}$).
- `Transition Space`: Compression corridor ($H=3.2\text{m}$) connecting rooms.
- `Turn Interval`: Straight path distance between two consecutive $90^\circ$ turns in corridors ($8.0\text{m} - 14.0\text{m}$).

### 7. RULES

- `[RULE-ARC-001]`: **Rigid Height Matrix**: Every room volume MUST match the vertical height $H$ assigned in Section 8 (Table 8.1) with tolerance $\pm 0.0\text{m}$.
- `[RULE-ARC-002]`: **Spatial Hierarchy Composition**: Every level MUST contain exactly:
  - $1$ Dominant Space (Hall or Gym)
  - $2 \text{ to } 4$ Secondary Spaces (Classrooms or Labs)
  - Interconnecting Transition Spaces (Corridors or Stairwells)
- `[RULE-ARC-003]`: **Compression & Release Delta**: Transition from a Compression space ($H=3.2\text{m}$) to a Release space ($H \ge 5.0\text{m}$) MUST occur across a single doorway threshold with height delta $\Delta H \ge 1.8\text{m}$.
- `[RULE-ARC-004]`: **Corridor Turn Intervals**: Straight path distance between two $90^\circ$ rectangular turns in corridors MUST fall within $[8.0\text{m}, 14.0\text{m}]$.
- `[RULE-ARC-005]`: **Sightline Visibility**: From any entrance doorway to a primary or secondary space, the exit doorway MUST be visible in direct line-of-sight across $D_{sightline} \ge 6.0\text{m}$.

### 8. ALGORITHMS

#### Table 8.1: Architectural Height & Dimension Matrix

| Space Category | `ModuleType` | Height ($H$) | Min Width ($W$) | Min Depth ($D$) | Wall Thickness |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Dominant | `SchoolGym` | $6.0\text{ m}$ | $16.0\text{ m}$ | $24.0\text{ m}$ | $0.50\text{ m}$ |
| Secondary | `SchoolClassroom` | $3.8\text{ m}$ | $8.0\text{ m}$ | $10.0\text{ m}$ | $0.30\text{ m}$ |
| Secondary | `SchoolLab` | $3.8\text{ m}$ | $12.0\text{ m}$ | $10.0\text{ m}$ | $0.30\text{ m}$ |
| Transition | `SchoolCorridor` | $3.2\text{ m}$ | $3.0\text{ m}$ | $8.0\text{ m}$ | $0.30\text{ m}$ |
| Transition | `SchoolStairwell` | $7.6\text{ m}$ | $8.0\text{ m}$ | $8.0\text{ m}$ | $0.50\text{ m}$ |
| Void | `SchoolCourtyard` | $8.0\text{ m}$ | $24.0\text{ m}$ | $24.0\text{ m}$ | $0.50\text{ m}$ |

### 9. CONSTRAINTS
- `[CONS-ARC-001]`: Prohibido non-orthogonal wall angles ($\ne 90^\circ$ or $180^\circ$) in base school architecture.
- `[CONS-ARC-002]`: Prohibido varying ceiling height inside a single room module without structural support columns.

### 10. VALIDATION
- `[VAL-ARC-001]`: `LevelValidator.cs` asserts $H_{classroom} == 3.8\text{f}$ and $H_{corridor} == 3.2\text{f}$ across all instantiated modules.
- `[VAL-ARC-002]`: Raycast analyzer verifies direct line-of-sight $D_{sightline} \ge 6.0\text{m}$ between entry and exit doors.

### 12. FAILURE CASES
- `[FAIL-ARC-001]`: **Height Matrix Violation**: A classroom is instantiated with $H=3.2\text{m}$. Result: `LevelValidator` flags `FAIL-ARC-01`.
- `[FAIL-ARC-002]`: **Occluded Sightline**: A wall blocks line-of-sight between entrance and exit doorway. Result: `FAIL-ARC-02`.
